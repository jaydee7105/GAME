## Full description (4000 chars max)

Drop. Merge. Grow the Sun!

Planet Drop is a relaxing, wonderfully satisfying physics puzzle. Drop adorable planets into a glass jar. When two of the same planet touch, they merge into a bigger one: Asteroids become Moons, Moons become Mercury, then Mars, Venus, Earth, Neptune, Uranus, Saturn, Jupiter... all the way up to the Sun.

Don't let the jar overflow! Planets that stay above the line for too long end the game.

★ 100 ADVENTURE LEVELS
Journey from the Asteroid Belt to the Solar Core across 10 chapters. Every level has its own goals and a limited number of drops: finish with drops to spare to earn all three stars. Beat the boss level at the end of each chapter!

★ EASY TO LEARN, HARD TO MASTER
Drag to aim, let go to drop. One finger is all you need, but planning the perfect chain reaction takes real skill.

★ CHAIN REACTIONS
One drop can set off merge after merge. Chains multiply your score.

★ SUPERNOVA!
Merge two Suns for an explosive supernova and a huge bonus.

★ POWER-UPS
Stuck? Feed a planet to a Black Hole, or Shake the whole jar to shuffle things up. Earn more by making Saturn, Jupiter and the Sun.

★ DAILY CHALLENGE
A fresh puzzle every day: the same planets, in the same order, for everyone. Build your streak!

★ ACHIEVEMENTS & JAR STYLES
22 achievements to earn, each with a reward. Unlock glowing Neon, Golden Rings, Comet Ice, Aurora, Solar Flare and Galaxy jars.

★ DISCOVER EVERY PLANET
Fill your planet collection and learn a fun space fact about each one.

★ BEAUTIFUL & CALM
Hand-crafted 3D planets, gentle ambient music and soothing sounds. Play for one minute or one hour.

★ FREE & FAIR
Free to play, no sign-ups, no paywalls and no energy timers. Plays offline. Out of room? Watch an optional video for a second chance.

How far can you grow your solar system?
