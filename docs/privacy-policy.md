# Planet Drop: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Planet Drop is a single-player offline game.

**We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.**

- The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
- The game does not request internet access.
- Your scores, settings and progress are saved only on your own device, in the
  app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- Android's optional "vibrate" permission is used only for haptic feedback,
  which you can turn off in the game's settings.

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, export
or delete on your behalf.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if online leaderboards are added),
the new version will be published here with a new date.

Questions? Contact the developer at the support email listed on the game's
