# Planet Drop: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Planet Drop is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts and no
sign-ups. It is free to play and is paid for by advertising.

## What stays on your device

Your scores, levels, stars, achievements, settings and saved game are stored
only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive them.
Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads served by **Google AdMob**: a banner at the bottom of
the screen while you play, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and an
optional video you can choose to watch to continue a game.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID (and app set ID),
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
- ad interactions (which ads were shown and tapped) and diagnostics (crashes,
  performance).

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
Data is encrypted in transit.

**Your choices**

- If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or another region where consent
  is required, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form
  before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in
  **Settings > Ad privacy choices**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads,
  in your device's Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings >
  Privacy > Ads).
- Ads are only requested while your device is online. The game is fully
  playable offline.

## Notifications

If you turn on the **Daily reminder**, the app schedules one local
notification a day on your device. No server is involved and nothing is sent
anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

## Sharing

If you tap **Share**, your score is handed to the app you pick (messages,
social apps...). We don't see what you share.

## Permissions

- Internet / network state: to load ads.
- Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
- Notifications: only for the optional daily reminder.
- Vibrate: haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

## Children

Planet Drop is not directed at children under 13 and its ads are limited to
content suitable for a general (PG) audience. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
date.
